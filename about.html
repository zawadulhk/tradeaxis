About TradeAxis Global Ventures

Building trusted connections through global trade, sourcing excellence, and reliable supply chain solutions across international markets.

Foundation & Scope

A Premier Gateway in International Commerce

Established in 2020, TradeAxis Global Ventures is a diversified international trading company dedicated to connecting businesses with reliable global markets through strategic sourcing, export, import, indenting, and supply chain solutions.

Headquartered in Bangladesh, TradeAxis serves as a trusted business partner for companies seeking dependable access to international products, industrial materials, specialized packaging solutions, and customized sourcing opportunities.

Our expertise spans multiple industries, including jute products exports, pharmaceuticals, plastics, packaging, food processing, textile, printing, consumer goods, and industrial manufacturing. Through our growing global network of manufacturers, suppliers, and business partners across Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and North America, we help organizations source quality products that meet their operational and commercial requirements.

One of our core strengths is pharmaceutical packaging solutions, where we support manufacturers with a wide range of conventional and specialized packaging materials, including advanced anti-counterfeit solutions designed to enhance product security, brand protection, and supply chain integrity.

Our Heritage

Our Journey & Evolution

From a vision for global trade to a growing international business network connecting continents.

2020

Company Establishment & Foundation

TradeAxis Global Ventures was established in 2020 with a clear vision: to create a reliable bridge between global suppliers and businesses seeking quality products, competitive sourcing, and dependable supply solutions. What started as a focused trading initiative began expanding its cross-border relationships across Asia.

2021 – 2023

Expansion Into Industrial Verticals & Pharma Packaging

Rapid expansion across 5 dedicated divisions: Export, Import & Indenting, Industrial Machinery, Local Trading, and HealthBridge. Built direct relationships with verified manufacturers across China, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Europe, and the United States.

Today & Future Horizon

Global Sourcing Powerhouse & Sustainable Export Leadership

Providing international businesses seamless access to industrial polymers, security packaging technologies, sustainable export goods, and turnkey machinery solutions with complete supply chain assurance.
