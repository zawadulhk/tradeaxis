Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information Collection

TradeAxis Global Ventures collects business contact information submitted voluntarily through our quotation requests, partnership application forms, and contact inquiries. This includes names, company designations, corporate emails, telephone numbers, and technical requirement specifications.

2. Use of Information

We use collected data solely to respond to commercial sourcing requests, formulate quotation tenders, execute indenting agreements, coordinate international shipping, and provide relevant industry updates. We never sell or commercially lease your corporate information.

3. Information Sharing

Information is shared only with verified manufacturer partners, logistics carriers, customs authorities, and financial institutions as required to fulfill commercial trade operations, or when mandated by law.

4. Data Security & Storage

We deploy industry-standard technical and operational measures to protect corporate communications against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

For inquiries regarding data stored or to request record removal, contact our data protection desk at privacy@tradeaxisgv.com.
