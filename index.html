Who We Are

A Trusted Partner in International Trade & Business Solutions

Established in 2020, TradeAxis Global Ventures is a Bangladesh-based international trading company committed to delivering comprehensive export, import, indenting, and sourcing solutions to businesses locally and globally.

Our expertise extends across diverse industries, including pharmaceuticals, plastics, packaging, food processing, textile, printing, consumer goods, and industrial manufacturing. Through our extensive supplier network and market knowledge, we support businesses in sourcing quality materials, products, and equipment from trusted international manufacturers.

Our core strength lies in understanding industry requirements and providing customized solutions rather than simply supplying products. Whether it is pharmaceutical-grade packaging materials, plastic industry raw materials, specialized industrial machinery, or sustainable jute products, we ensure reliability, efficiency, and long-term collaboration.
