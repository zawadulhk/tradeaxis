Operating Verticals

Our Business Divisions

Integrated trading solutions and specialized verticals tailored to address the requirements of international buyers, manufacturers, and industrial customers.

TradeAxis Global Ventures operates through specialized business divisions designed to combine deep technical industry knowledge with global supply chain efficiency. From sustainable natural exports to advanced pharmaceutical barrier films and heavy production equipment, each division functions with meticulous quality control.

Division 01

Export Division

Bringing Bangladesh's Sustainable Products to Global Markets

Our Export Division specializes in sourcing and supplying high-quality jute products from Bangladesh, one of the world's leading producers of natural golden jute fiber.

We work directly with certified artisan production centers capable of producing standard catalog items as well as custom private-label designs for international department stores, home décor brands, and eco-lifestyle retailers in North America, Europe, and Asia.

Export Product Portfolio:

🌿 Handwoven Jute Rugs
👜 Retail & Fashion Bags
🧺 Braided Laundry Baskets
🖼️ Artisanal Wall Décor
🍽️ Placemats & Table Runners
✨ Customized Private Label
Division 02

Import & Indenting Division

Reliable Global Sourcing for Industrial Raw Materials & Specialized Inputs

Our Import & Indenting Division connects manufacturing enterprises with premier petrochemical refiners, masterbatch producers, and fine chemical manufacturers across South Korea, China, Thailand, Vietnam, and Europe.

We operate both as a direct importer and an authorized indenting agent, negotiating manufacturer-direct pricing, arranging LC financing parameters, securing factory allocations, and ensuring batch-to-batch consistency.

Key Sourcing Domains:

🧪 Virgin Polymer Resins (HDPE/PP/PET)
🎨 Color & Additive Masterbatches
📦 BOPP, OPP Tape & Packaging Films
🛡️ Pharmaceutical Cleanroom Blister Foils
🥛 Food Industry Milk Powders & Stabilizers
⚙️ Specialty Chemical Additives
Division 03

Industrial Machinery Division

Customized Machinery Solutions From Global Manufacturing Partners

Whether upgrading existing production capacity or establishing turnkey industrial facilities, TradeAxis provides end-to-end industrial machinery procurement tailored to customer specifications.

Our extensive manufacturing networks in China, Thailand, and Europe allow us to identify top-tier equipment builders, conduct pre-shipment factory acceptance testing (FAT), and support seamless shipping, clearance, and installation coordination.

Machinery Categories:

⚙️ Plastic Extrusion & Injection Molding
📦 Automated Packaging & Cartoning Lines
💊 Pharma Blister Packaging Machines
🍦 Food & Dairy Processing Equipment
🖨️ Flexographic & Rotogravure Printers
🤖 Automated Robotic Systems
Division 04

Local Trading Division

Supporting Industries Through Reliable Domestic Supply Solutions

Our Local Trading Division provides businesses across Bangladesh with prompt, dependable access to vital packaging substrates, industrial consumables, polymer raw materials, and promotional corporate items.

By maintaining reliable local inventory and strong relationships with domestic mills and converters, we help clients overcome sudden supply chain disruptions and reduce inventory holding costs.

  • ✓ Rapid Domestic Delivery: Immediate dispatch from strategically located hubs in Bangladesh.
  • ✓ Flexible Order Quantities: Supporting both small-batch industrial orders and continuous large-scale supply contracts.
  • ✓ Local Currency Billing: Streamlining domestic procurement without international currency exposure.
Specialized Venture

HealthBridge by TradeAxis

Connecting Patients With Trusted International Healthcare Providers

HealthBridge is an independent international healthcare initiative established by TradeAxis Global Ventures to bridge the gap between patients seeking specialized medical care and world-class medical institutions across the globe.

Through institutional coordination agreements with JCI-accredited hospitals in Thailand, Singapore, Turkey, and China, HealthBridge assists patients with doctor matching, medical cost estimations, visa assistance, and end-to-end medical travel logistics.
