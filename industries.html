Industrial Support

Industries We Support

Providing specialized trading solutions, technical materials sourcing, and machinery across key manufacturing and commercial sectors.

Core Focus Sector

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

Certified high-barrier packaging materials, anti-counterfeit security solutions, and automated processing machinery for pharmaceutical manufacturers.

Key Solutions:

  • Blister aluminum foils (push-through & cold-form Alu-Alu)
  • Cleanroom PVC and PVDC barrier films
  • Holographic anti-counterfeit security labels
  • Tablet compression, blister packing, and cartoning machinery
Chemical & Polymers

Plastics & Polymer Processing

Prime virgin polymer resins and specialty masterbatches engineered for injection molding, blow molding, and high-speed film extrusion.

Key Solutions:

  • Polyethylene resins (HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE) & Polypropylene (PP Homo & Copolymer)
  • Color masterbatches: high-opacity white, carbon black & custom shades
  • Performance additives: UV stabilizers, anti-static & filler masterbatch
  • Extrusion lines and heavy-tonnage injection molding machinery
Packaging Industry

Packaging & Converting

High-performance barrier films, specialty substrates, and precision converting machinery for modern flexible packaging converters.

Key Solutions:

  • BOPP films (plain, heat-sealable, matte, and metalized barrier grades)
  • OPP tape jumbo rolls for domestic slitting converters
  • Heat transfer printing films and premium In-Mould Labels (IML)
  • High-speed slitter rewinders and automated pouch-making machinery
Dairy & Frozen

Ice Cream & Dairy Manufacturing

Functional texture-modifying ingredients, imported dairy solids, and commercial processing systems for dairy processors and ice cream brands.

Key Solutions:

  • Emulsifier-stabilizer blends engineered for overrun and meltdown resistance
  • Imported Full Cream Milk Powder (FCMP) and Skimmed Milk Powder (SMP)
  • Continuous ice cream freezers and automated hardening systems
  • In-Mould Labeled tubs and protective barrier packaging
🖨️

Textile & Printing

Heat transfer films, rotogravure printing presses, OPP adhesive tapes, and packaging substrates for textile mills and print houses.

🍞

Food & Beverage

Hydrocolloids, food stabilizers, clean-label ingredients, and sanitary processing lines for commercial food producers.

🛍️

Consumer Goods & Retail

Handcrafted braided jute rugs, woven storage baskets, and private label tote bags for global lifestyle retailers.

🌾

Agriculture & Jute

Biodegradable agricultural geotextiles, nursery wrapping jute, and natural fiber packaging for farming distributors.

Connecting international markets through reliable sourcing, export-import excellence, and customized supply chains.
