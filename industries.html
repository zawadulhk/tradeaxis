Industrial Support

Industries We Support

Providing specialized trading solutions, technical materials sourcing, and machinery across key manufacturing and commercial sectors.

Core Focus Sector

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

The pharmaceutical industry demands the highest level of regulatory compliance, barrier integrity, and tamper security. We support domestic and regional drug manufacturers with certified packaging materials and machinery.

Key Solutions Supplied:

  • Blister packaging aluminum foils (printed and plain push-through)
  • Cold-form Alu-Alu high-barrier laminates for moisture-sensitive APIs
  • Cleanroom-manufactured PVC and PVDC blister films
  • Advanced anti-counterfeit holographic solutions and tamper-evident security labels
  • Automated blister packing, cartoning, and tablet compression machinery
Chemical & Polymers

Plastics & Polymer Processing

We supply plastic convertors and manufacturers with continuous, cost-effective access to prime virgin polymers and high-performance masterbatches tailored for injection molding, blow molding, and extrusion lines.

Key Solutions Supplied:

  • Polyethylene resins: HDPE (blow & pipe), LDPE & LLDPE film grades
  • Polypropylene: PP Homopolymer (raffia/tape) and PP Copolymers
  • High-opacity White Masterbatch and jet-black carbon concentrates
  • UV stabilizers, anti-static, anti-block, and calcium carbonate filler masterbatch
  • Plastic extrusion lines and heavy tonnage injection molding machinery
Packaging Industry

Packaging & Converting

Modern consumer goods require aesthetic excellence and robust barrier protection. We supply converting houses and flexible packaging printers with superior film substrates and equipment.

Key Solutions Supplied:

  • BOPP films (Plain, Heat-Sealable, Matte, and Metalized barrier grades)
  • OPP tape jumbo rolls for domestic tape slitting converters
  • Heat transfer printing films and premium In-Mould Labels (IML)
  • High-speed slitter rewinder machinery and pouch-making units
Dairy & Frozen

Ice Cream & Dairy Manufacturing

We supply industrial dairy processors and ice cream brands with critical texture-modifying functional ingredients, high-grade milk solids, and specialized commercial production equipment.

Key Solutions Supplied:

  • Integrated emulsifier-stabilizer blends engineered for melt resistance and overrun
  • Imported Full Cream Milk Powder (FCMP) and Skimmed Milk Powder (SMP)
  • Continuous ice cream freezers and automated hardening equipment
  • In-Mould Labeled ice cream tubs and tamper-evident barrier packaging
🖨️

Textile & Printing

Supporting textile mills, garment exporters, and print houses with specialized heat transfer films, rotogravure printing presses, OPP adhesive tapes, and packaging materials.

🍞

Food & Beverage

Functional hydrocolloids (Xanthan gum, Guar gum), food stabilizers, clean-label ingredients, and sanitary food processing lines for bakery and beverage industries.

🛍️

Consumer Goods & Retail

Supplying global department stores, lifestyle brands, and homeware distributors with handcrafted jute home décor, woven rugs, laundry baskets, and private label tote bags.

🌾

Agriculture & Jute

Providing biodegradable agricultural geotextiles, nursery wrapping jute, packaging twine, and natural fiber products to international farming and horticultural distributors.
